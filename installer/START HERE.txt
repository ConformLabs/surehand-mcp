SUREHAND MCP - START HERE
=========================

Surehand MCP for Adobe After Effects (formerly AE MCP) lets Claude, ChatGPT (desktop app),
Cursor or Codex control Adobe After Effects on your computer.

Pick ONE way to install:


>> Only use Claude Desktop?  Open the folder "1. Claude Desktop (easiest)"
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Double-click  Surehand-MCP.mcpb
   Claude Desktop opens and asks to install it. Click Install.
2. Restart After Effects (once).
3. In Claude, ask: "Check After Effects is connected."


>> Mac (any AI app, including ChatGPT)?  Open the folder "2. Mac"
-----------------------------------------------------------------
1. Double-click  "1. Install"
2. Restart After Effects (once).
3. In Claude, ask: "Check After Effects is connected."
   Optional: double-click "2. Test Installation" with After Effects open.


>> Windows (any AI app, including ChatGPT)?  Open the folder "3. Windows"
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Double-click  "1. Install"
2. Restart After Effects (once).
3. In Claude, ask: "Check After Effects is connected."
   Optional: double-click "2. Test Installation" with After Effects open.

Node.js is downloaded automatically if your computer doesn't have it.
If Claude Desktop is open during the install, the installer offers to restart it.
ChatGPT desktop app: restart it after installing. "after-effects" then appears in
ChatGPT under Settings > Plugins > Servers. Make sure its switch is on.


WHAT THE NUMBERED FILES DO (in the Mac and Windows folders)
-----------------------------------------------------------
1. Install             Installs everything and connects your AI apps.
2. Test Installation   Run with After Effects open: every line should show a check mark.
                       It makes a temporary comp and removes it; your project is not changed.
3. Connect ChatGPT     Only for ChatGPT on the WEB (chatgpt.com). The ChatGPT desktop app
                       doesn't need it. Follow the instructions it shows and keep the
                       window open while you use it.
4. Uninstall           Removes everything. (If you used Surehand-MCP.mcpb, also remove it in
                       Claude Desktop > Settings > Extensions.)

The "Support files" folder is used by the files above; you don't need to open it.


IF YOUR COMPUTER BLOCKS THE FILES
---------------------------------
Mac:     "cannot be opened because it is from an unidentified developer"
         -> Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, click "Open Anyway",
            then double-click the file again. (Only needed the first time.)
Windows: "Windows protected your PC" -> click "More info" > "Run anyway".


UPDATES & HELP
--------------
When a new version is out, After Effects shows it in Window > Extensions > Surehand MCP.
Something wrong? Run "2. Test Installation"; if it finds a problem it prints a report
(with no passwords or keys in it) that you can send along with your question.
New versions and questions: https://github.com/ConformLabs/surehand-mcp
