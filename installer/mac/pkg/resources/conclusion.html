Surehand MCP is installed

  1. Restart After Effects if it is open. The connection starts automatically with it.
  2. Restart your AI apps (Claude Desktop, ChatGPT, Codex, Cursor) if they are open, so they pick up the new connection. If Claude Desktop was open during the install, quit it fully (Cmd+Q) and open it again.
  3. Ask your AI app: "Check After Effects is connected, then make a 1080p comp with an animated title."

A log of this install is in ~/.surehand/ae/install.log. If something did not connect, it says what and why.

To check the installation, uninstall, or connect ChatGPT on the web, see github.com/ConformLabs/surehand-mcp.
