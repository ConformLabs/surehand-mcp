AFTER EFFECTS MCP - START HERE
==============================

Lets Claude (or ChatGPT, Cursor, Codex) control Adobe After Effects on your computer.

Pick ONE way to install:


>> Only use Claude Desktop?  Open the folder "1. Claude Desktop (easiest)"
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Double-click  AE-MCP.mcpb
   Claude Desktop opens and asks to install it. Click Install.
2. Restart After Effects (once).
3. In Claude, ask: "Check After Effects is connected."


>> Mac (any AI app, including ChatGPT)?  Open the folder "2. Mac"
-----------------------------------------------------------------
1. Double-click  Install
2. Restart After Effects (once).
3. In Claude, ask: "Check After Effects is connected."


>> Windows (any AI app, including ChatGPT)?  Open the folder "3. Windows"
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Double-click  Install
2. Restart After Effects (once).
3. In Claude, ask: "Check After Effects is connected."

Node.js is downloaded automatically if your computer doesn't have it.
If Claude Desktop is open during the install, the installer offers to restart it.


OTHER BUTTONS (in the Mac and Windows folders)
----------------------------------------------
Test Installation   Run with After Effects open: every line should show a check mark.
                    It makes a temporary comp and removes it; your project is not changed.
Connect ChatGPT     Connects ChatGPT. Follow the instructions it shows and keep the window open.
Uninstall           Removes everything.
                    (If you used AE-MCP.mcpb, also remove it in Claude Desktop >
                    Settings > Extensions.)

The "Support files" folder is used by the buttons above; you don't need to open it.


IF YOUR COMPUTER BLOCKS THE FILES
---------------------------------
Mac:     "cannot be opened because it is from an unidentified developer"
         -> Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, click "Open Anyway",
            then double-click the file again. (Only needed the first time.)
Windows: "Windows protected your PC" -> click "More info" > "Run anyway".
